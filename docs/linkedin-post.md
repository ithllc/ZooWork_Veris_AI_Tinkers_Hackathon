I had a great time at the AI Tinkerers Fashion Track hackathon, meeting wonderful people across the fashion industry and tech. The conversations about sourcing, sustainability, and what agents can actually help with were a highlight.

I built RevampAI to turn garment records and supplier claims into evidence-focused reviews. Real ZooWork agents flag missing documentation and produce manager reports, while ZooData adds fashion product-market context. You can chat with the agent, use voice input in supported browsers, and sign up for an inbox and optional browser alerts. MCP and Agent2Agent interfaces let other assistants submit records and retrieve results.

It’s a hackathon project, not a certification authority: people make the final sourcing decisions.

Try the site: https://revampai-3ms5.onrender.com

Explore, fork, and try the open-source AGPL-3.0 code: https://github.com/ithllc/ZooWork_Veris_AI_Tinkers_Hackathon
